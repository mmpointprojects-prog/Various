# Spotter — Guide (v0.1.0, built 2026-10-13 05:53 UTC)

Spotter is a personal Android workout app. It knows today's workout, logs every set, gives you a
weight-and-rep alternative when you can't do an exercise, lets you split a day, times your sets and
rest, keeps a calendar of everything, and runs your daily check-ins, peptide/supplement reminders
and weekly weigh-ins.

## 1. Install on your phone (no Play Store)

1. On your phone, open this repo on GitHub → `apk/` folder → tap the `Spotter_….apk` file → **View raw** / **Download**.
   (Or, once GitHub Actions runs: repo → **Releases** → newest `Spotter_…` → tap the `.apk`.)
2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask to allow installs from your browser/Files app → **Allow** (one time).
3. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect warns that it's an unknown app → **Install anyway** (it's your own build).
4. Open Spotter → **Allow notifications** when asked.
5. **Important on Samsung / Xiaomi / OnePlus / Pixel battery saver:** Settings → Apps → Spotter → Battery →
   **Unrestricted**. Otherwise the phone may delay your 8 AM check-in and dose reminders.
6. In Spotter → Settings → **Test alarm in 1 min**, then lock the phone. If it buzzes, reminders work.

**Updating:** install a newer APK over the old one. Your data is kept, because every build is signed
with the same key (`keystore/`). Don't uninstall first: uninstalling deletes your data. Use
Settings → **Export** for a backup before big changes.

## 2. First-time setup

1. **Your program.** Pick one:
   - **Build my own splits.** Name each split (e.g. *Chest & Triceps*), pick its weekdays, and add exercises
     (90+ built in, or add custom). Set sets × rep range × rest for each.
   - **Give me a plan from my goals & time.** Choose goal (strength / muscle / fat loss / general), days per week (2–6),
     minutes in the gym (30–90), and equipment. It builds the splits and fills the time you have. You can edit it after.
   - Choose **By weekday** (Chest & Tri every Monday) or **Next in rotation** (whatever day you go, you get the next split).
2. **Check-ins & reminders.** Units, morning check-in (default 8:00 AM), evening check-in (default 9:00 PM),
   weigh-in day and time, and how long before the gym you want the snack reminder (default 60 min).
3. **Peptides & supplements.** Optional now, or later from the **Supps** tab.

## 3. Daily flow

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| **8:00 AM** (your time) | Notification: *How do you feel?* Rate feeling, hours slept, soreness and stress, say whether you're training, and give your gym time. You get recovery/vitamin suggestions, and Spotter schedules a **snack reminder** (gym time − 60 min) and a **"gym in 15 min"** reminder. |
| Dose times | Notification per peptide/supplement, with **Taken / Snooze 15m / Skip** buttons right on it. Peptides show syringe units and the next injection site if you turned that on. |
| At the gym | **Start workout** → readiness check (energy, sleep, soreness in today's muscles) → **Full workout**, **Split it**, or rest. |
| **Evening** | Evening check-in → recovery suggestions for tonight, based on how the day went and whether you train tomorrow. |
| **Weigh-in day** | Notification at your chosen time → log your weight → chart. |

## 4. During a workout

- Each exercise shows a **target**: weight × reps, based on your last session (double progression):
  - Hit the top of the rep range on every set → weight goes up one step (5 lb; 10 lb on barbell leg lifts).
  - Otherwise → same weight, beat your reps.
  - Fell well short → drops one step.
- **Start set timer** (optional) times the set. **Log set** saves it and starts the **rest countdown**.
  The countdown keeps running with the screen off, and your phone buzzes when rest is over. +30s / Skip rest are available.
- **Can't do this** → pick a reason (equipment taken, pain, too fatigued, not at this gym) → you get a list of
  alternatives, same movement first, each with a suggested **weight × reps**:
  - from **your own history on that alternative**, if you've done it before; otherwise
  - **converted from your last performance on the exercise you can't do** (e.g. barbell bench 200 × 10 →
    dumbbell bench ~70s per hand), shaved 10% because it's a new movement.
- **Do it later** moves an exercise to the end (machine taken). **Skip** keeps any sets already logged.
- A **New PR** banner appears when you beat your estimated 1-rep max.

### Splitting a day (your Chest & Triceps example)
At the readiness check, tap **Split it** → select **Triceps** → **Start Triceps**. Chest is saved as
**"Finish later: Chest"** on the Today screen. Tap **Start now** whenever you're ready, later that day or another day.
You can also tap **Finish → Finish & save the rest for later** mid-workout.

## 5. Calendar
Dots for each day: workout done, workout planned, doses taken, weigh-in. Tap a day to see each session's
**start time, stop time and duration**, every set, any swaps, readiness scores, check-ins, and every dose (taken / skipped / missed).

## 6. Peptides & supplements
For each one: type, name, dose and unit, times per day, schedule (every day / specific weekdays / every N days),
start date, optional **cycling** (e.g. 8 weeks on, 4 off), instructions (e.g. "fasted, 30 min before food"),
and for peptides a **vial calculator** (mg in vial + mL water + mcg dose → units on a U-100 syringe and doses per vial),
**injection-site rotation**, and a **doses-left counter** that warns when you're at 3 or fewer.

> Spotter never recommends peptide or medication doses. Enter the protocol from your prescriber.
> Recovery and vitamin suggestions are general wellness info, not medical advice.

## 7. Backup
Settings → **Export** saves a `.json` backup (e.g. to Google Drive). **Restore** loads one.
Data lives only on your phone otherwise.

## 8. Changing the app later
- Code: Kotlin + Jetpack Compose, stock Material 3 theme. Main parts:
  - `app/src/main/java/com/mmpoint/spotter/logic/`: progression, alternatives, plan generator, recovery rules, dose schedules
  - `.../data/ExerciseLibrary.kt`: built-in exercises (add more here)
  - `.../notify/Reminders.kt`: all notifications/alarms
  - `.../ui/screens/`: every screen
- Build locally: `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug` → `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`.
- Every push to this branch also builds an APK on GitHub Actions and posts it under **Releases**.

## 9. Known limits (v0.1)
- Switching lb ↔ kg doesn't convert weights you've already logged.
- The snack and "gym soon" reminders depend on that day's morning check-in (that's where your gym time comes from).
- **Can't do this** is offered before the first set of an exercise. Once sets are logged, use **Skip** (keeps those sets) and add a replacement with **+ Add an exercise**.
- Tested with unit tests plus a scripted run of the full app UI (Robolectric). Not yet tested on a physical phone; that's step one.
