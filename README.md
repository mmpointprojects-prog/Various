# Spotter: personal workout app (Android)

Knows today's workout, logs sets/reps/weight, gives history-based alternatives when you can't do an
exercise, lets you split a day and finish it later, times sets and rest, keeps a workout calendar, and
runs morning/evening check-ins, pre-gym snack timing, peptide/supplement reminders and weekly weigh-ins.

- **Install:** download the APK from [`apk/`](apk/) (or the latest **Release**) on your phone and open it.
- **Full guide:** [`docs/Spotter_Guide_2026-10-13_0553.md`](docs/Spotter_Guide_2026-10-13_0553.md)
- **Build:** `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug`
