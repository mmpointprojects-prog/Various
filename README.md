# Personal Android apps

## Spotter: workout app
Knows today's workout, logs sets/reps/weight, gives history-based alternatives when you can't do an
exercise, lets you split a day and finish it later, times sets and rest, keeps a workout calendar, and
runs morning/evening check-ins, pre-gym snack timing, peptide/supplement reminders and weekly weigh-ins.

- **Install:** download the APK from [`apk/`](apk/) (or the latest **Release**) on your phone and open it.
- **Full guide:** [`docs/Spotter_Guide_2026-10-13_0553.md`](docs/Spotter_Guide_2026-10-13_0553.md)
- **Build:** `./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:assembleDebug`

## TubeNotes: YouTube transcripts, summaries and bullet points
Share a YouTube link to it and choose **Full transcript**, **Brief summary** or **Bullet points**. Every
video and result is kept in a searchable log.

- **Install:** [`apk/TubeNotes_2026-10-29_0258.apk`](apk/TubeNotes_2026-10-29_0258.apk) (or the latest `tubenotes-…` **Release**).
- **Full guide:** [`docs/TubeNotes_Guide_2026-10-29_0234.md`](docs/TubeNotes_Guide_2026-10-29_0234.md)
- **Build:** `./gradlew :tubenotes:testDebugUnitTest :tubenotes:assembleDebug`
